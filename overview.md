# Overview

## Event Background

 - Organise a one-day Campus Sustainability Fair at Ngee Ann Polytechnic.
 - The event aims to encourage students to adopt environmentally friendly habits.
 - The fair will feature interactive activities, educational booths and sustainability challenges.
- The event is planned for 15 March 2027 at the school atrium.
- The target audience is approximately 200 students from different schools within the polytechnic.

## Event Objectives

- Raise awareness of environmental issues and sustainable living.
- Encourage students to reduce waste and recycle more.
- Promote teamwork through interactive group activities.
- Provide opportunities for students to learn practical ways to protect the environment.
