# Budget

## Assumptions

- Expected attendance is 80 students.
- The event runs for about three hours.
- The venue is a campus space provided free of charge by the student union.
- All amounts are in Singapore dollars (SGD) and are estimates only.
- Volunteers from the organising team will run the event, so no staff fees are included.

## Possible funding sources

- Student union event grant.
- Small contribution from a sponsoring department.
- Optional low-cost ticket price if funding falls short.

## Questions for discussion

- Is 80 attendees a realistic estimate? Refreshment costs depend on it.
- Should we apply for the grant before finalising the programme, or after?
- Can any items (such as equipment) be borrowed rather than hired?
